Everything you need to play on Unstable Events, in one install.

![What this is](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/notzyvex1/unstableeventsmodpackimages/main/headers/01_what_this_is.png)

Unstable Events is a Minecraft server, and this is the pack we play it with. Instead of
hunting down mods one at a time and hoping they're the right versions, you install this and
join. It's built around the server: the resource pack that makes our custom items and menus
look right is already in here and already switched on.

Here's the thing vanilla doesn't tell you: a lot of Unstable Events is invisible on a plain
client. Our custom blocks, items and menus show up as placeholders or don't render at all,
and the world past a few chunks is just fog. You're walking around a server you can't
actually see.

This pack fixes that. The server's textures load the way they were drawn, custom models and
animated blocks render properly, and because the whole thing is built on Sodium you can push
render distance far past what vanilla manages and still hold your framerate. Most people end
up seeing more of the map at a higher FPS than they had before installing anything.

Nothing in here changes how the game plays. No new blocks, no new recipes, no magic system —
it's the stuff that makes the server visible and the game smooth, plus voice chat so you can
actually talk to people. You can join without it. You just won't see much.

The Unstable Events pack is the server's own — custom items, purple HUD, themed menus. It is enabled out of the box, so there is nothing to turn on.

Unstable Events, Fullbright UB, Tiny Tools, Chat Reporting Helper, Mod Menu Helper, Sodium Translations

![Requirements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/notzyvex1/unstableeventsmodpackimages/main/headers/03_requirements.png)

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Java 21 or newer
- 4 GB allocated is comfortable. 2 GB works if you skip shaders.

![Getting started](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/notzyvex1/unstableeventsmodpackimages/main/headers/04_getting_started.png)

1. Install it through the Modrinth App, or import the `.mrpack` into Prism or ATLauncher.
2. Launch once and let it finish downloading.
3. Join the server. The resource pack is already on.

Shaders are optional — drop any Iris shader into `shaderpacks` and pick it in Video Settings.
